# 069-08 Recursive Plonky3 Epoch Aggregation

<objective>
Implement strict storage-owned epoch statement/proof/manifest schemas and a real
recursive Plonky3 proof over exactly the configured ordered canonical range,
with exact transition count, verified chunk/range coverage, and an independent
canonical predicate proof rather than Nova-only wrapping. Add a separate
cross-epoch rolling history proof
so bounded proof retention is a proven theorem rather than a storage assumption.
Every recursion layer must use the pinned Plonky3 STARK/FRI/hash-Merkle profile,
verify its child proof in-circuit, and terminate without an EC/pairing wrapper.
The repository MUST expose one canonical local proof-backend package named
`z00z_plonky3_circuit_prover`. It owns both the audited circuit prover and the
used FRI-recursive verifier so their proof/prover-data types cannot diverge.
There is no local or external `p3-recursion` package in the resolved storage
graph, no `p3-circuit-prover` compatibility identity, and no dependency alias,
wrapper, or shim. The unused WHIR/sumcheck recursion surface is out of scope.
</objective>

## canonical_production_epoch_contract

- The Phase-069 production cadence is exactly one canonical recursive Plonky3
  epoch proof envelope per consecutive `2000` finalized checkpoint blocks,
  equal to `10,000` seconds (`2 h 46 min 40 s`) at five-second finalization.
  Shorter ranges are smoke fixtures only and cannot close this plan.
- The complete canonical encoded per-epoch Plonky3 payload has a production
  target of `2,097,152` bytes (`2 MiB`) before transport compression. It contains
  the complete canonical `EpochRangeStatementV2`, one final
  `Plonky3HistoryProofV2` body whose circuit verifies the exact epoch child, all
  public inputs, manifest data, and framing. The verified child epoch proof body
  is an internal recursion input and MUST NOT be duplicated in the exported
  envelope. The limit is not per child, per root, or per proof type.
- The finite publication cap for the same complete payload is `4,194,304`
  bytes (`4 MiB`). A verified `(2 MiB, 4 MiB]` envelope is published with
  `Plonky3ProofSizeStatusV2::TargetMissed`, remains valid recursion/history
  input, and cannot close the Plan-08 performance/size acceptance gate.
- Only the final envelope is publishable. Base leaves, chunk proofs, the
  standalone epoch child proof, predecessor history proofs, and intermediate
  recursion nodes are ephemeral and MUST NOT be published, permanently
  retained, or split across multiple final objects to evade the `4 MiB` cap. An
  intermediate proof larger than `2 MiB` may remain internal and undergo
  another sound recursive-compaction layer within its separate resource-safety
  bound.
- `16 MiB` is solely the fail-closed parser/allocation and DoS ceiling for
  untrusted input. A canonical payload in `(4 MiB, 16 MiB]` MUST return
  `ProofSizeBudgetExceeded`; payloads above `16 MiB` reject at generic ingress.
- Size compliance MUST NOT be obtained by weakening field, FRI, hash,
  grinding, query, transcript, recursion, replica, or residual-security
  parameters. If the exact `2000`-block proof cannot meet both the `2 MiB`
  limit and the inherited security/resource contracts, Plan 08 remains
  `BLOCKED`.
- A final envelope above `4 MiB` rejects as `ProofSizeBudgetExceeded`; it does not
  panic the node, invalidate already-finalized canonical blocks, or stop Nova
  folding. The epoch stays in the bounded pending-PQ queue for a materially
  changed sound compaction attempt. At the configured pending limit, the
  canonical action is
  `stop_compression_publication_keep_fold_finality`, never silent dropping,
  multi-envelope publication, or consensus/finality shutdown.

## live_epoch_frontier_contract

- One immutable `EpochFrontierAuthorityV2` is the single epoch-start proof
  authority and binds the
  fixed 2000-block range, start state, and all config/predicate/parameter/
  verifier/direct-AIR/frontier/security generations. It deliberately excludes
  future transition, end-root, close-anchor, and Nova values. One
  `EpochProofWorkManifestV2` closes exactly once after transition 2000 and
  binds that authority plus every ordered statement/artifact/link/delta/
  witness/challenge/DA commitment, adjacent state transition, exact end root,
  epoch close anchor, and optional Nova chain root.
- A production frontier leaf is one bounded `EpochTraceChunkV2` over an exact
  domain row/event interval and block-ordinal range. It is not a complete
  `Plonky3BaseProofV2` for one block. The chunk statement binds the immutable
  epoch-start authority digest, exact ordered transition-slice commitment,
  boundary states and
  running lookup/permutation/root accumulators.
- An early chunk MUST NOT bind the digest of the still-incomplete 2000-block
  work manifest. That digest is unknowable before epoch close and would make
  continuous proving impossible. Actual-verified chunks may enter a tentative
  frontier under the epoch-start authority; the closed manifest must
  independently recompute and match every ordered slice before any root can be
  sealed or published.
- Completion is asynchronous: canonical finality never waits for proof work,
  but every chunk result MUST pass the actual pinned Plonky3 verifier before it
  enters `EpochProofFrontierV2`.
- Verified results may arrive out of order. The frontier persistently indexes
  exact contiguous ranges and schedules a parent only for adjacent compatible
  boundaries. A parent is installed only after its actual pinned-verifier
  result and dependency-proof digests verify; child bodies remain reachable
  until that installation is crash-durable.
- Generation-static opcode semantics, constant tables, and circuit common data
  are prepared once per authority generation. Epoch-static identities are
  bound once per work manifest. Block-dynamic transaction, hash, JMT, witness,
  and link rows stream through bounded chunks. Cache and shape identity MUST
  bind only the exact relevant slice and boundary statement; unrelated bytes
  from another domain or block MUST NOT invalidate reusable common shape.
- Production chunks use direct table-oriented KoalaBear AIRs for canonical
  trace framing, packed byte/limb ranges, SHA-256 compression, JMT path/update
  micro-ops, uniqueness/list permutation, typed-event commitments, and
  inter-block continuity. Passing every block through the complete Plan-07
  general `CircuitBuilder` theorem, or concatenating 2000 copies of that
  circuit, is prohibited. Plan-07 remains the differential/mutation oracle
  against which the direct epoch tables are checked.
- Cross-chunk public boundary accumulators enforce exact ordering, lookup/
  permutation continuity, inter-block root/link continuity, and final closure.
  Missing, duplicate, overlapping, reordered, mixed-generation, or
  proof-digest-substituted chunks fail closed.
- Restart MUST recover the same generation-bound chunks/ranges and
  idempotently resume only missing work. Directory enumeration, current mutable
  config, or re-proving already accepted chunks MUST NOT define authority.
  The successor rotates frontier, circuit, and cache generations; old
  complete-block frontier bytes reject and are never translated.
- At the exact `2000`-block boundary, proof availability remains
  non-authoritative. Epoch close may complete only missing bounded chunks and
  remaining range folds. It MUST NOT begin 2000 calls to the complete Plan-07
  base prover.
- Plan 08 owns the canonical frontier grammar, persistence, actual-verifier
  admission, and local worker seam. Plan 10 owns authenticated local/remote
  job dispatch, leases, cancellation, reorg quarantine, independent
  cgroup/host budgets, and
  restart scheduling. Until Plan 10 passes, the Plan-08 seam is local-only and
  production distributed proving is not live.
- Plan-07 `Plonky3BaseProofV2` remains the single-transition differential,
  mutation, and bounded smoke theorem. It MUST NOT be the production leaf
  repeated once per block. Epoch trace chunks are also not HJMT storage leaves;
  the existing HJMT shard dispatcher is not recursive-proof scheduling
  evidence.

## resource_feasibility_and_granularity_contract

- The exact production path MUST construct one epoch-wide streamed proof graph.
  A loop that invokes `Plonky3BaseAdapterV2::prove_and_verify` once for every
  block is a terminal architectural blocker even if it is restartable.
- The exact one-transaction fixture currently expands one complete block proof
  into 186 domain coordinates per replica, three replicas, 558 base domain
  proofs, and 557 base recursive parents/folds. Repeating that topology for
  2000 blocks plus the epoch layers creates approximately 2,234,001 logical
  proof constructions. This graph is explicitly rejected.
- The measured Plan-07 theorem also lowers one transition to
  `1,103,499,762` general ALU rows. Merely concatenating 2000 current circuits
  would preserve roughly `2.2 trillion` ALU rows and is explicitly rejected.
  Bounded persistence alone is not an optimization; the production epoch path
  must replace the dominant bit-circuit lowering with audited direct AIR
  tables and lookup/permutation links while preserving the exact theorem.
- The exact fixture MUST build the 2000 canonical transitions once in order.
  Reconstructing prefixes `1..=h` for every target height performs 2,001,000
  fixture transition iterations and is explicitly rejected.
- At five-second finalization the supported topology receives `0.2`
  transitions per second. Continuous proving evidence MUST sustain at least
  that rate with declared headroom and bounded backlog; a terminal proof that
  consumes more than one epoch interval is not production acceptance even if
  it eventually verifies.
- One exact-2000 epoch/history theorem MUST pass the actual pinned verifier
  without swap within the isolated worker's two-hour limit. The real prover
  process target remains at most 16 GiB peak RSS and the emergency cgroup
  ceiling remains 24 GiB.
- No feasibility change may weaken field, FRI, hash, grinding, query,
  transcript, recursion, replica, or residual-security parameters, and Nova
  verification cannot replace any transition constraint.
- The accepted root-cause analysis and successor boundary are authoritative in
  `069-08-ARCHITECTURE-ROOT-CAUSE.md`.
