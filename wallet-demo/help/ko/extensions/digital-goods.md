## Overview

Digital Goods issues a bounded licence, download, plugin, or other digital
entitlement. It represents utility rights and does not turn content into an NFT.

**Boundary:** a Z00Z Extension does not control the wallet. It proposes a typed
action. Wallet checks scope, builds the package, requests confirmation, and
only then passes it to the settlement path.

## How to use this view

Choose the provider, exact item or licence, allowed uses or devices, validity,
and transferability. Wallet reviews the provider and entitlement scope before
issuance.

## Terms and controls

| Term or control | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | Publisher or delivery service named by the entitlement. |
| Item or licence | Exact software, file, software add-on, or digital right. |
| Uses or devices | Maximum activation or presentation count. |
| Validity and transfer | Time and transfer ceilings. |
| Evidence | Entitlement digest plus delivery or redemption receipt. |

## Safety and limits

The right grants only the declared item or use. It does not execute downloaded
code, verify content safety or quality, or prove that a provider delivered the
content.
